Our Mission

Blue Marine is dedicated to protecting and restoring life in the ocean — tackling overfishing and driving the creation of marine reserves that work for nature and people.

From the Solent to the Maldives, we unite fishers, scientists and coastal communities to rebuild biodiversity and secure a thriving ocean for future generations.
